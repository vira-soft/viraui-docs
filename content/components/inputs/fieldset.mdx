---
title: Fieldset
description: Groups related radios or checkboxes with a legend and optional group helper copy.
---

`Fieldset` wraps Base UI `Fieldset.Root` and `Legend` with Vira legend typography and optional group-level description and error slots — use it whenever a cluster of [`Radio`](/components/inputs/radio) or [`Checkbox`](/components/inputs/checkbox) options needs a visible group name and shared helper copy. Pass `render` to merge the fieldset root with `RadioGroup` or `CheckboxGroup` so the group and legend stay one accessible unit. Group validation and `name` still live on `Field.Root` above the fieldset; individual options keep their own labels.
