Effects add motion and atmosphere around your layout without replacing structural components. Beam and Glow wrap a card in a transparent shell so the highlight tracks the surface radius; ProgressiveBlur and StaticNoise sit as overlays on scroll regions or panels; Typewriter animates inline copy for hero lines. They stay decorative — pair with real content inside [Surface](/components/layout/surface) or [Text](/components/typography/text), and respect [prefers-reduced-motion](/foundation/motion) where the component already gates animation.
