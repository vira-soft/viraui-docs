---
title: ProgressiveBlur
description: Multi-layer edge blur — fades from full backdrop blur at one edge to clear at the opposite side.
---

ProgressiveBlur stacks several masked `backdrop-filter` layers so blur strength ramps along one edge — `direction` picks which edge starts at full blur (`top`, `right`, `bottom`, or `left`). `blur` sets the maximum radius at that edge; `easing` and optional `steps` control how the internal layers distribute the falloff (exponential is the default curve). The root is `aria-hidden` — it is a visual scrim, not interactive content.

Mount it at the edge of a scroll region or over media when content should fade under a soft veil instead of a hard clip. Parent needs something to blur through — a photo, gradient, or scrolling list behind the overlay. Tune `steps` only when banding shows at the default layer count for your chosen easing.

A bottom-edge blur over a scrolling feed so the last items dissolve under a footer action.
