---
title: Glow
description: Pointer-following border highlight — proximity, spread, and optional rainbow or global gradient attachment.
---

Glow wraps content the same way [Beam](/components/effects/beam) does: a transparent [Surface](/components/layout/surface) shell with `radius`, and the visible card as a child Surface with `radius="auto"`. A document-level tracker reads pointer position relative to each registered root and writes a continuous highlight angle — `angleLag` controls how softly the arc follows (set `0` for instant snap). `proximity` is the distance from the edge where the glow reaches full strength; `spread` sets the arc width in degrees. Resting `opacity` keeps a faint rim visible when the pointer is far away.

`borderWidth`, `borderColor`, and `borderOffset` shape the masked ring under the gradient. `glowColor` overrides the theme conic highlight; `rainbowColors` switches to a multi-stop palette and disables shared attachment. `globalHighlight` keeps one fixed gradient across cards when several Glow instances sit on the same page — turn it off when each card should feel independent. `fitContent` shrink-wraps the shell to the child.
