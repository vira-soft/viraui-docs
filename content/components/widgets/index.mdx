---
title: Widgets
description: Compact display primitives — identity, status, labels, progress, and decorative callouts without full actions or form fields.
---

Widgets surface small, read-mostly information on dense screens — who someone is, whether something is new, what category a row belongs to, how full a quota is, or where to look in a marketing layout. They sit beside actions and inputs: an [Avatar](/components/widgets/avatar) beside a name is not a button, a [Badge](/components/widgets/badge) dot on an icon is not a notification center, and [Meter](/components/widgets/meter) shows progress without accepting drag input like [Slider](/components/inputs/slider). Reach for this category when the UI needs a lightweight visual affordance that should not steal focus or submit a form.
