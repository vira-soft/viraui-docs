---
title: Badge
description: Wraps content with an animated corner status dot — toggle show, position, highlight color, and dot diameter.
---

Badge wraps any child and optionally paints a decorative status dot on a corner. The child stays fully interactive — common pairings are [Avatar](/components/widgets/avatar), [IconButton](/components/actions/icon-button), or [Chip](/components/widgets/chip) — while `show` drives whether the dot and radial mask cutout appear. `position` picks a corner (`top-end` by default); `color` maps to a foundation highlight token via `--vui-badge-color`; `size` sets dot diameter through `--vui-badge-size`. Enter and exit animate the hole and dot in sequence, and the animation collapses immediately when `prefers-reduced-motion` is on. The dot is decorative — pair it with visible copy or an accessible name on the wrapped control when the status matters to everyone, not only sighted users who notice the glyph.
