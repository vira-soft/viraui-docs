---
title: ToggleGroup
description: Coordinated single- or multi-select pressed states across ToggleButton children with group labeling.
---

ToggleGroup wires related ToggleButton items into one selection model. Each child needs a `value` and an accessible name; the root accepts `value`, `defaultValue`, and `onValueChange` as `string[]`, plus `multiple` for multi-select toolbars. Default layout is a zero-gap Stack (`row` when horizontal, `column` when vertical) — your agent owns spacing around the group. Label the set with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` on the group root so assistive tech understands the cluster.

## Common examples

A horizontal segmented toolbar with exclusive pressed values across ToggleButton children is the baseline group pattern.
