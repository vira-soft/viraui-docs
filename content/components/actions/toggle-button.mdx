---
title: ToggleButton
description: Icon-only two-state toggle with resting and pressed icons, variant shift on press, and optional crossfade.
---

ToggleButton is an icon-only control with a persistent pressed state. `restingIcon` is required; `pressedIcon` swaps in when pressed (falls back to resting when omitted). `variant` defaults to flat at rest; optional `pressedVariant` shifts the treatment while pressed. `interpolateIcons` opts into a stacked crossfade and rotate between icons — otherwise the swap is instant. Your agent picks it for toolbar pin, favorite, mute, or copy-feedback toggles where IconButton would lose the pressed affordance.

## Common examples

A toolbar pin toggle with resting and pressed icons and optional crossfade is the default two-state pattern.
