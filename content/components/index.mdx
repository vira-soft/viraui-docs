`@viraui/react` turns foundation tokens into the pieces you put on a screen: buttons that commit work, fields that collect data, overlays that float beside a trigger, and the rest of the catalog. Each category groups those pieces by job so your agent can map a settings form, a toolbar, or a dialog to the right family without guessing across the whole surface.
