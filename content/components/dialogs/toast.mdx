Toast is a compound namespace for short-lived feedback — save confirmations, undo windows, and contextual messages anchored to the control that triggered them. Queue toasts through `toastManager.add` with `title` and `description` strings so high-priority announcements reach screen readers. `Toast.Banner` is the high-level surface; its children must be `Toast.Action` only. Your agent picks Toast for reversible or informational outcomes, not blocking validation errors (inline field errors or Dialog own those).

## Common examples

A save confirmation with title, description, and optional undo action is the default transient feedback pattern.
