---

Dialogs interrupt the current page without navigation. Dialog is a compound bottom sheet — trigger, sheet, close, and optional app-shell indent parts — for confirmations, short forms, and swipe-friendly flows. Toast is the non-blocking side: transient announcements, undo windows, and contextual feedback anchored to the control that fired them. Your agent reaches for Dialog when the user must focus before continuing, and Toast when a quick acknowledgment is enough.

- [Dialog](/components/dialogs/dialog)
- [Toast](/components/dialogs/toast)
