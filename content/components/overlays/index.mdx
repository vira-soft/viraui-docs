title: Overlays

---

Overlays in ViraUI float above the page, anchored to a trigger you compose with `render`. Each compound owns portal mounting, positioning, dismiss behavior, and keyboard contracts — you wire `Trigger` plus the matching surface (`Popup`, `Sheet`, or `Content`) instead of stacking raw portals. Pick the shape by intent: **Menu** for command lists and toggles, **Popover** for richer non-modal panels (forms, detail sheets), **Tooltip** for brief supplementary hints. Modal work belongs on [Dialog](/components/dialogs/dialog); stacking relative to nearby surfaces follows [Elevation](/foundation/elevation).
